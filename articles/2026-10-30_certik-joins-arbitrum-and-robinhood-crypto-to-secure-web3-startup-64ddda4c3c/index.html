CertiK Joins Arbitrum and Robinhood Crypto to Secure Web3 Startups

Presence Editorial · October 30, 2026 · 2 min read

CertiK has partnered with Arbitrum and Robinhood Crypto to assist early-stage Web3 startups in mitigating security risks prior to market launch by joining the Arbitrum Mentorship Program. This collaboration offers technical resources, security assessments, and expert mentorship aimed at helping founders navigate the complexities of developing and launching blockchain products. The initiative emphasizes integrating user protection and security considerations from the earliest stages of development, alongside support for product creation, fundraising, and market entry strategies.

Under the terms of this partnership, participating teams will gain access to CertiK's AI Auditor and Skynet tools, discounted security assessments, and personalized guidance covering security, technology, business development, and go-to-market strategy. These resources are designed to enable founders to identify potential vulnerabilities within their applications and facilitate more informed decision-making throughout the development process.

Partnership Launches Security Support Initiative

For blockchain ventures, maintaining robust security is essential, as flaws within smart contracts or application frameworks risk causing monetary harm to users and tarnishing a venture's standing. Tackling these dangers at the outset enables groups to refine their offerings prior to release, avoiding dependence on final inspections right before going live.

Winning cohorts will receive further assistance from the initiative, such as prolonged use of AI Auditor, expedited booking for pre-deployment reviews, compliance advice suited to specific regions, and marketing chances. CertiK's involvement augments the wider aid provided by Arbitrum and Robinhood Crypto via this mentorship effort. Through uniting security knowledge with network ties, the scheme intends to furnish nascent groups with tangible assets needed to handle the technical and business requirements of constructing Web3 enterprises.

Tools and Guidance for Early-Stage Teams

CertiK Co-Founder and CEO Ronghui Gu stated that the collaboration between Arbitrum and Robinhood Crypto creates significant opportunities for early-stage teams to expand while prioritizing future user safety. He expressed satisfaction with contributing to this initiative by assisting founders in addressing security concerns at an early stage to establish a robust foundation for sustained growth.

The partnership emphasizes integrating security as a core component of the startup development lifecycle. Through the combination of specialized security tools, professional assessments, and mentorship, CertiK will enable participating founders to incorporate risk management into their product preparation for launch and subsequent expansion.
