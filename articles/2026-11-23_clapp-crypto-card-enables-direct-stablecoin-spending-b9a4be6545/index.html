Clapp Crypto Card Enables Direct Stablecoin Spending

Presence Editorial · November 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Visa has identified more than 160 stablecoin-powered crypto card programs by October 2026, marking a period where payment volume increased nearly 200% compared to the previous year. According to figures cited by a16z, monthly spending through these cards, which primarily utilize USDT and USDC, reached approximately $759 million in July, representing roughly 2.5 times the level recorded a year earlier.

Clapp Finance has introduced its own product, the Clapp Crypto Card, which allows users to spend funds from their USDT or USDC balances at any merchant that accepts Visa. Holders maintain their assets in stablecoins while the card automatically manages the necessary conversion for each purchase transaction.

Market Growth in Crypto Payments

A standalone wallet holding stablecoins lacks utility at typical retail terminals or hospitality counters. The Clapp card accesses funds directly from a user's USDT or USDC reserves, performing the conversion to fiat currency at the transaction moment. Sellers receive payment in their local money without prior exchange steps or the need for a dedicated funding account.

Consider an independent contractor receiving compensation in USDC who then reserves accommodation in Lisbon. The reservation platform demands euros; the card utilizes the USDC holding and settles the hotel in that currency without any prior sale on an exchange. This solution serves individuals maintaining significant stablecoin assets who prefer not to liquidate them for routine spending, effectively integrating their current holdings into a familiar payment infrastructure.

Clapp Finance enables users to create a virtual card instantly within its app, eliminating the wait for physical plastic and allowing immediate online purchases. By adding this card to Apple Pay or Google Pay, holders can execute contactless transactions at millions of Visa merchants.

The Clapp Crypto Card operates in more than 150 countries for both online and in-person use, serving travelers as effectively as residents. Account holders may adjust spending limits, freeze or unfreeze the card instantly, and confirm online payments with a one-time password (OTP), measures that provide enhanced security for crypto-linked balances even when funds are held in USD-pegged stablecoins.

A monthly software subscription represents a typical scenario in which setting a spending limit slightly above the renewal price prevents overcharging, and the card can be frozen in seconds if a merchant changes terms or if something appears irregular.

Clapp does not charge its own fee for converting crypto to fiat at checkout, nor is there a monthly card fee; however, payments are not entirely free as other fees, including standard Visa network and payment processor charges, may apply depending on the transaction. The one-time issuance cost is $9.99, currently reduced to $4.99.

Even so, the pricing structure remains straightforward without a Clapp conversion fee or monthly charges, making it particularly suitable for users who intend to utilize the card frequently rather than merely keeping it as a backup.

The card operates within a broader ecosystem where Clapp Finance integrates its wallet and exchange with savings and staking features, alongside multi-collateral credit lines that enable users to access liquidity without liquidating their crypto assets; for instance, a holder of USDT or USDC can retain assets on the exchange, earn rewards through savings, and spend from the card's balance all within a single application, while also enjoying Visa Platinum benefits such as extended warranties on eligible purchases and 180 days of shopping protection.

Stablecoins are transitioning from tools used primarily for trading and cross-border transfers into instruments for daily spending. Until recently, utilizing stablecoins for ordinary purchases required multiple manual steps and faced limited merchant acceptance, but holders can now simply tap their phone or watch at checkout. With the introduction of cards like Clapp's, businesses no longer need to directly accept cryptocurrency, while customers avoid having to explain blockchain mechanics; the card network handles the merchant side while conversion occurs quietly on autopilot.

Instant Virtual Card Features

Individuals who already use Clapp or maintain USDT and USDC balances can utilize the Clapp Crypto Card to make purchases directly, bypassing the need for prior conversion. This approach benefits those receiving stablecoin payments or wishing to keep funds in digital currency form. The product functions as part of a larger platform where Clapp Finance combines its wallet and exchange capabilities with savings, staking options, and multi-collateral credit lines that provide liquidity without requiring asset liquidation; specifically, a user holding USDT or USDC may keep assets on the exchange, earn rewards via savings, and spend from the card balance within one app while receiving Visa Platinum perks like extended warranties on qualifying items and 180 days of shopping protection.

Current Clapp account holders and individuals maintaining USDT or USDC balances can utilize the Clapp Crypto Card to make purchases directly, eliminating the need for prior conversion. This solution benefits recipients of stablecoin payments or those wishing to retain assets in digital dollars. Consolidating the wallet and exchange functions into a single location removes the necessity of maintaining a distinct account solely for transaction purposes. Users must verify spending limits and regional availability for their specific country.
