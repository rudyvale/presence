0G Foundation Faces Disclosure Scrutiny After Upbit Warning

Presence Editorial · November 24, 2026 · 4 min read

Following a warning from South Korean cryptocurrency exchange Upbit, the 0G Foundation declared an examination of its transparency measures. This action comes after Upbit classified the organization's native token as an investment caution asset and halted new deposits, citing issues regarding how information about token distribution and circulation strategies was disclosed.

Deposits involving the 0G token were frozen by the South Korean platform on November 24, affecting all three associated trading pairs. The foundation committed to auditing its records, correcting inconsistencies, and improving how it shares data with investors. Upbit plans to evaluate this status until the end of December, noting that trading privileges may be revoked if the outstanding issues are not settled.

Upbit Flags 0G Over Disclosure Concerns

According to an announcement released by Upbit on November 24, the exchange flagged potential issues with how critical data is shared by the entity managing or issuing the project. These concerns involve information that might significantly impact cryptocurrency pricing or sway investor choices. The warning applies to every one of the three 0G trading pairs available on the platform—specifically 0G/KRW, 0G/BTC, and 0G/USDT—and the limitations began at 3 p.m. Korean Standard Time.

Following this development, the 0G Foundation issued a statement via X confirming awareness of Upbit's ruling. The organization emphasized that its immediate focus is conducting a thorough examination of the circumstances and associated records. Furthermore, the foundation reiterated its dedication to offering more clarity, correcting inconsistencies, and improving disclosure mechanisms to foster enhanced transparency and responsibility.

Upbit listed multiple reasons for its choice, such as the prompt release of data regarding token creation, distribution strategies, operational blueprints, and safety breaches impacting investors. The platform noted that its evaluation considered if key details were adequately shared, how much prior plans shifted, and if the methods managing those shifts were open and fair.

Following its analysis, Upbit concluded that the situation might endanger investors and marked 0G as a cautionary trading asset for protection. The exchange referenced Article 17 within South Korea's Virtual Asset User Protection Act Enforcement Decree in its public notice.

Upbit has set aside the first review window to last until the end of December's fourth week, spanning from Dec. 21 through Dec. 24, a time dedicated to investigating the situation regarding its digital asset trading support termination policy designation. The final result of this evaluation might lead the exchange to lift the warning, extend the assessment timeline, or halt all trading support for the specific cryptocurrency.

If the problems uncovered during the investigation remain unaddressed, the platform may proceed with ending trading support, an action that will be accompanied by a distinct notice outlining the relevant timetable. In addition to issuing the trading caution, Upbit has directed clients to refrain from moving 0G tokens into their exchange wallets after deposit functions were suspended.

Following the suspension of deposit capabilities, Upbit directed users to stop moving 0G tokens into their exchange wallets. The platform clarified that any deposits occurring after the cutoff point will not be added to customer balances but will enter a return process. Upbit cautioned that recovering these funds might fail under specific conditions, and individuals waiting for their tokens back could face delays until deposit functions are restored, given that the relevant return mechanisms are currently paused.

Concerning the timing of transactions, Upbit stated that wallet infrastructure processing levels and network congestion can delay how quickly transfers appear in customer accounts, even after blockchain confirmations. While addressing these disclosure issues, the foundation affirmed that its strategy to build decentralized artificial intelligence infrastructure stays intact, with staff members advancing efforts in product creation, ecosystem growth, and research.

According to the foundation, reliability stems from steady behavior, open dialogue, and tangible outcomes. This perspective emerged after 0G Labs debuted its Aristotle mainnet in September 2025, a moment that coincided with the debut of its native coin and its appearance on multiple prominent trading platforms such as Binance, Kraken, and Upbit.
