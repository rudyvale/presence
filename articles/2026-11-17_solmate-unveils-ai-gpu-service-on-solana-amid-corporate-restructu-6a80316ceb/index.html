Solmate Unveils AI GPU Service on Solana Amid Corporate Restructuring

Presence Editorial · November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Solmate Infrastructure has declared its intention to provide GPU-as-a-Service for artificial intelligence tasks, expecting clients to settle payments in US dollars or stablecoins via the Solana network. The firm aims to procure this computing power from locations in the UAE to serve enterprises requiring AI processing capabilities.

Negotiations regarding agreements for GPU access are ongoing, leaving specific pricing details and starting capacity levels unannounced. This entity listed on Nasdaq currently manages a Solana treasury alongside its blockchain infrastructure operations. The November 16 declaration from Solmate outlines an asset-light model that links customers with computing resources for AI training and inference, consolidating sourcing, contract management, delivery logistics, and specialized software offerings into a single commercial partner.

Asset-Light Model Targets AI Workloads

Solmate is in talks to secure GPU access and will reveal details regarding capacity deals, client agreements, and the rollout of its initial computing assets once each step finishes. Specific rental pricing, named customers, or the exact count and variety of GPUs available have not been made public by the firm.

According to its opening statement, Solmate positions this venture as a global solution for acquiring the processing power required for AI training and deployment. Based in the UAE, the entity highlighted its Nasdaq status and lean operational model, while Solana's channel noted that transactions could occur using dollars or stablecoins.

According to Solmate CEO Ron Sade, the sectors of artificial intelligence and digital assets are merging, a trend that places the firm's emerging infrastructure at their crossroads. This new venture is connected to the company's current holdings in SOL tokens. Official communications clarify that the enterprise remains in development, with expectations that computing power from the United Arab Emirates will become available once commercial deals are finalized.

Solmate characterizes its present activities on its official site as institutional support for the Solana network, featuring validators designed to enhance connectivity originating from Abu Dhabi. Financial records from November 15 indicate a treasury holding of approximately 1.26 million SOL, valued at $90.5 million based on a Kraken price of $71.85 recorded at 4 p.m. New York time; the organization notes these figures are unaudited estimates.

According to the dashboard, realized staking rewards totaled 62,000 SOL between Jan. 1 and Oct. 31; this number does not include projected earnings or rewards that have accrued but remain unrealized. Solmate's business model involves raising funds through stock offerings and direct placements while running validators in the UAE to earn income via staking, decentralized finance strategies, and infrastructure fees.

The company trades on Nasdaq under the ticker SLMT and lists Anchorage Digital, BitGo, and Kraken Financial among its custody providers on its website. In March, Solmate outlined plans for a UAE infrastructure hub alongside proposals to reorganize its corporate identity and capital structure.

Solmate Infrastructure, formerly operating under the legal name Brera Holdings, proposed adopting its new corporate identity while retaining the SLMT ticker to align with its blockchain business. This March announcement followed a September 2025 financing round that introduced a Solana treasury and infrastructure strategy alongside the company's soccer investments, backed by $300 million in private capital from ARK Invest, RockawayX, and the Solana Foundation.

Leaders outlined intentions to gather SOL tokens and build validator and staking capabilities in Abu Dhabi as part of a restructuring plan that also involved shutting down two struggling soccer franchises while keeping the Italian side Juve Stabia active. In other parts of the network, the Solana Foundation launched private AI utilities with Nito on Nov. 13, providing an open-source toolkit enabling apps to verify hardware records on Solana while AI computation occurs off-chain.

Corporate Identity Shift and Treasury Status

While the Solana Foundation's beta service functions on the devnet and validates attestations for Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP, verification for NVIDIA GPUs is still being developed. The Foundation labeled this launch as unaudited and designed exclusively for developers. Concurrently, Akash Network brought its AKT token to Solana via Sunrise after a Nov. 12 announcement; the platform serves as a decentralized marketplace enabling developers and AI firms to lease computing power from independent hosts.

Holders can transfer their AKT assets from Cosmos to Solana using a migration portal set to stay active for two years, maintaining a one-to-one exchange rate. However, shifting Akash's computing marketplace operations necessitates a distinct governance vote.
