Crypto ETFs Face $1.53 Billion Weekly Exodus as Bitcoin Drains Lead

Presence Editorial · November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

From November 9 to November 13, U.S.-based exchange-traded funds monitoring assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Hyperliquid, and Zcash saw a combined net outflow of $1.53 billion, driven primarily by exits from Bitcoin-linked products. BlackRock's IBIT fund specifically posted withdrawals across every one of the five trading days, while the broader Bitcoin ETF category absorbed $1.065 billion in redemptions.

Although Ethereum ETFs managed to return to a modest net inflow by Friday, they still saw a reduction of $398.9 million for the week; meanwhile, Solana and Zcash funds registered losses of $32 million and $30.8 million respectively over the same period. Hyperliquid ETFs posted $4.4 million in net outflows during this time, a figure that represents a decrease from $15.7 million recorded the previous week.

Bitcoin Dominates Withdrawals Amid Market Selloff

According to Farside Investors, all five tracked fund groups concluded the week with net withdrawals totaling $1,531,400,000 when summing daily figures, a decrease from $1,700,600,000 recorded across the same groups during Nov. 2–6.

Withdrawal reductions varied across the week, as Bitcoin and Hyperliquid funds experienced smaller losses compared to the prior period, whereas Ethereum, Solana, and Zcash funds registered higher net outflows. The five tracked groups saw combined withdrawals drop by $169,200,000 relative to the previous week, marking a decrease of approximately 9.9%. Bitcoin comprised nearly 70% of this latest aggregate total, with Ethereum trailing at roughly 26%.

The sales occurred alongside declines in the underlying digital assets, as Bitcoin dipped below $69,000 on Nov. 13 and Ether fell beneath $2,000 during that same market downturn. Bitcoin exchange-traded funds experienced net outflows each day, extending their streak of negative sessions to seven through Friday, with weekly withdrawals dropping approximately 16.9% from the prior week's $1.282 billion.

The $201 million outflow on Monday was succeeded by $95 million on Tuesday, while Wednesday saw withdrawals hit $299.8 million—the highest daily total for the week—before figures moderated to $284.1 million on Thursday and $185.4 million on Friday. BlackRock's IBIT fund lost $908.4 million across the five trading sessions, Fidelity's FBTC shed $111.3 million, and Morgan Stanley's MSBT gained $54.4 million, recording positive flows for the first four days with no net movement on Friday.

Across the two November trading weeks, Bitcoin ETFs have lost $2.347 billion, while Farside's cumulative net-flow total remained positive at approximately $54.7 billion through Nov. 13. Ethereum ETFs finished Friday with a positive result after recording a weekly loss of $398.9 million. Ethereum ETFs concluded Friday with $2.3 million in net inflows, ending a six-session streak of withdrawals. Fidelity's FETH attracted $11.6 million, which countered $9.3 million exiting Grayscale's ETH fund.

The small recovery followed four negative sessions earlier in the week, with Monday producing $184.6 million in withdrawals, Tuesday losing $1.3 million, Wednesday shedding $144.8 million, and Thursday recording $70.5 million in outflows. Weekly losses reached $398.9 million, approximately 9.2% higher than the prior week's $365.2 million; BlackRock's ETHA contributed $311.1 million to the most recent withdrawals, while net flow was zero on Tuesday and Friday.

Bitcoin and Ethereum funds collectively experienced a loss of $1.4642 billion, representing approximately 95.6% of the total withdrawals across the five monitored groups during the week. Within this category, FETH declined by $12.1 million despite some buying activity on Friday, while BlackRock's ETHB fund shed $25 million and Grayscale's ETH recorded net withdrawals totaling $43 million.

Solana funds saw net outflows of $32 million, a figure that compares to $25 million in the previous week, with every one of the five trading sessions ending in negative territory. Daily withdrawal amounts decreased after reaching a peak of $13.4 million on Tuesday, and specifically for Bitwise's BSOL fund, weekly losses amounted to $12.4 million. This decline was partially mitigated by an inflow of $1.7 million on Thursday, which helped offset Monday's withdrawals of $5.9 million and Tuesday's outflows of $8.2 million.

FSOL shed $11.3 million, including $3.1 million on Friday. TSOL lost $2.8 million, GSOL recorded $2.4 million in withdrawals, and MSOL lost $2.1 million. VSOL and SOEZ posted smaller outflows of $300,000 and $700,000, respectively. No Solana fund ended the week with a positive net flow, despite BSOL recording one positive session.

Ethereum Rebounds Slightly After Sharp Decline

Zcash's ZCSH recorded $30.8 million in weekly withdrawals, compared with $13 million during Nov. 2–6. Wednesday's $14.6 million outflow was the largest, followed by $9.9 million Thursday and $4.1 million Friday. During the week, November withdrawals for ZCSH reached $43.8 million over the initial 10 trading sessions.

Hyperliquid ETFs experienced a net loss of $4.4 million over the week, with activity split between two funds as THYP shed $5.2 million via withdrawals on Monday and Wednesday while BHYP gained $800,000 on Monday, leaving HYPG with no net movement; the group posted zero flows on Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday following a prior week where it lost $15.7 million.
