DAOs, A Canon

Zoran Basich · 9 min read

What is a DAO, and what isn’t? Why do DAOs matter? How do DAOs fit within web3, crypto, the creator economy, the future of work, and many other domains?

Inspired by our NFT Canon earlier this year, and the original Crypto Canon, we’ve assembled the list below of resources for people looking to understand, build, and otherwise participate in these “decentralized autonomous organizations” — which point toward the future of community, coordination, work… and much, much more. If you have suggestions for strong pieces to include, let us know @a16z.

* * *
