Jane Street Swaps Bitcoin ETFs as Holdings Surge

Presence Editorial · November 13, 2026 · 4 min read

During the third quarter of 2026, Jane Street boosted its position in Fidelity's spot Bitcoin exchange-traded fund by nearly eight times, while simultaneously trimming its BlackRock IBIT share count by roughly 19%. The trading entity grew its Fidelity ETF stake by 691% to hold 7.01 million shares valued at $509.2 million, yet it lowered its BlackRock IBIT holdings by 18.7% despite the position's value climbing to $957.3 million.

The total value of Jane Street's reported stakes in FBTC, IBIT, and ARKB climbed to roughly $1.79 billion. This firm grew its ARKB allocation to 11.69 million shares and more than doubled its holdings in the BlackRock Ethereum ETF. In that same timeframe, JPMorgan added 9.6% to its IBIT position, whereas Laurore, based in Hong Kong, cut its stake by 33%.

Fidelity Stake Jumps Nearly Eightfold

Jane Street's November 12 Form 13F filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission indicates that the trading firm held 7,010,008 shares of Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC) as of September 30, a figure representing an increase from 886,736 shares recorded three months prior. This position was valued at $509.2 million, compared with $45.3 million at the end of June.

Jane Street's holdings in the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund grew substantially, adding 6,123,272 FBTC shares to its portfolio. This accumulation marks a roughly 691% rise compared to the amount held in the third quarter. By September 30, the firm owned nearly 7.9 times as many shares as it did in June, significantly expanding Fidelity's role within Jane Street's disclosed Bitcoin ETF assets.

The most recent adjustment reverses a portion of the firm's earlier reduction in its stake within Fidelity's fund, as Jane Street decreased its FBTC shareholding by approximately 60% during the first quarter when it trimmed its exposure to BlackRock's Bitcoin ETF. The second-quarter filing indicated a rise in BlackRock shares to approximately 24.88 million, with that position valued at $828.2 million as of June 30. By that time, Jane Street's aggregate Bitcoin ETF holdings had surpassed $1 billion, primarily driven by IBIT.

Data from the third quarter reveal a different distribution among the funds, showing that based on reported share positions for FBTC, IBIT, and the ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF (ARKB), their combined value at quarter-end rose from approximately $926 million in June to $1.79 billion in September.

Jane Street reduced its position in BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT) to 20,221,140 shares by the end of September, a decrease of 4,657,051 shares representing an 18.7% drop from the 24,878,191 shares held at June 30. Despite this reduction in share count, the reported value of the holding increased to $957.3 million from $828.2 million.

The filing also disclosed separate options tied to IBIT, showing that Jane Street held call positions for 34.97 million shares and put positions for 45.32 million shares. As of November 12, BlackRock recorded roughly $56.3 billion in net assets for the fund, keeping it as the top U.S. spot Bitcoin ETF by asset size.

Jane Street significantly increased its position in ARKB, expanding holdings from 2,700,151 shares at the end of June to 11,689,194 shares by September 30. This accumulation represented an addition of nearly 8.99 million shares over the quarter, resulting in a position valued at $323.7 million compared with $52.5 million previously.

The firm also expanded its stake in BlackRock's iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA), raising share counts from 7,606,021 to 15,438,249 between June 30 and September. This gain of approximately 7.83 million shares brought the ETHA stake value to about $310.6 million at September's close, up from $90.4 million recorded at the end of June.

BlackRock Position Shrinks Despite Value Rise

JPMorgan Chase's November 12 13F filing indicated that its holding in BlackRock's IBIT fund reached 11.41 million shares, valued at approximately $540 million, representing an increase of about 1 million shares or 9.6% from the prior quarter. In contrast to this growth in Bitcoin exposure, the bank's position in the BlackRock Ethereum ETF declined significantly, falling to just 399 shares from roughly 1.17 million at the end of June.

At the same time, Laurore, which is based in Hong Kong, decreased its IBIT holding by 1.6 million shares, leaving a total of 3,246,279 shares valued at roughly $153.7 million following a 33% reduction in share count for the quarter. This reporting came after Barclays disclosed a 48% rise in IBIT shares and Citigroup established a new FBTC position comprising 1.64 million shares as of September 30, with both financial institutions submitting their third-quarter data prior to Jane Street's filing on November 12.
