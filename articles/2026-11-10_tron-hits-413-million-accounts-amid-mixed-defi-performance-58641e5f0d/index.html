TRON Hits 413 Million Accounts Amid Mixed DeFi Performance

Presence Editorial · November 10, 2026 · 4 min read

On November 8, the TRON network crossed the threshold of 413 million total accounts, a development Justin Sun made public on November 10. While the platform maintains millions of daily active addresses and experiences consistent transaction increases, some segments of its decentralized finance sector are showing signs of reduced activity.

The network averaged 4,520,000 daily active accounts and 11,830,000 transactions over the past 30 days, with overall transaction activity growing by 2.51%. USDT transfers on TRON averaged approximately $24,860,000,000 per day during that month, representing an increase of 2.4% from the preceding period, while TRON's DeFi total value locked fell to approximately $4,960,000,000 and decentralized exchange trading volume declined by 73.68% over seven days.

Account Milestone and Daily Activity

Blockchain explorer TRONSCAN issued an announcement on November 8 stating that the network's cumulative account count had surpassed a significant milestone, a development underscored by Justin Sun, the founder of TRON, in an X post dated November 10. This achievement occurs as TRON maintains high volumes of stablecoin transfers, even though recent network data indicates a more varied trend regarding growth over the last month.

TRONSCAN data revealed that as of November 10, the blockchain hosted more than 413.5 million accounts, with roughly 173,000 new accounts generated in the previous 24 hours. The cumulative total reflects the number of blockchain accounts established since the network's inception rather than the count of individual users, as one person or organization may manage multiple addresses, ensuring accurate representation of unique wallet entities within the system.

Although the cumulative total of accounts keeps rising, TRON's active account metrics indicate that the network maintained an average of roughly 4.52 million daily active accounts throughout the last 30 days. Daily activity surpassed 5 million on November 5 and November 6 before dropping to 4.45 million on November 7, only to rebound to 4.84 million the next day, reflecting a fluctuating pattern of engagement across the reporting window.

TRONSCAN includes accounts with transfer records within the reporting window in its counts, encompassing both wallet and contract accounts. Transaction activity has stayed relatively consistent over this same timeframe, with the network handling an average of 11.83 million transactions per day. Based on its transaction statistics, the 30-day transaction count increased by 2.51% relative to the prior period.

On November 9, TRON processed a total of 12,990,000 transactions, which included approximately 4,790,000 TRX transfers and 2,300,000 USDT transfers. An additional 3,660,000 transactions were dedicated to resource delegation and reclamation, representing 28.15% of the day's total activity. Resource delegation enables users to allocate network resources to other accounts, allowing them to execute transactions without directly paying the full cost in TRX.

Stablecoin transfers remain a significant component of TRON activity, particularly via Tether's USDT. According to TRONSCAN statistics covering the past 30 days, the average daily transfer volume for USDT reached approximately $24,860,000,000, marking a 2.4% increase from the preceding period. During this timeframe, the number of accounts transferring USDT averaged roughly 1,050,000 per day, reflecting an 1.8% rise over the same comparison period.

Token Terminal's quarterly data indicates that TRON strengthened its standing in the stablecoin market by surpassing Ethereum in USDT supply during the third quarter. Specifically, TRON maintained an average holding of $90.75 billion in USDT, which exceeded Ethereum's average of $87.73 billion for the same period. In terms of network activity, the platform facilitated approximately $2.18 trillion in stablecoin transfers throughout the quarter, while its monthly active addresses averaged 17.81 million.

Nansen research provided a separate quarterly stablecoin settlement figure of $2.21 trillion, representing a 5.1% increase from the previous three months. Although Binance and OKX were identified by Nansen as accounting for considerable transaction activity among exchanges, the study did not isolate exchange transfers from other payment activities by dollar value.

According to DefiLlama data, TRON's decentralized finance ecosystem held approximately $4.96 billion in total value locked as of November 10, marking a decrease from roughly $5.58 billion recorded on October 12.

Stablecoin Dominance vs. DeFi Decline

During the identical interval, locked liquidity fell by nearly 11%, driving total value under $5 billion in early November. At that moment, TRON's stablecoin market cap hit roughly $94.16 billion, with USDT comprising 97.9% of the aggregate. More recently, decentralized exchange performance has softened, as DefiLlama logged about $389.46 million in trading volume across seven days, a drop of 73.68% from the prior week.

Financial inflows have encountered headwinds. Data from TRON's third-quarter report indicates transaction fees reached roughly $701.4 million, representing a 4% decline compared to the prior quarter. Token Terminal notes that the mean fee per transaction dropped to about $0.64 throughout this period, even as the aggregate number of transactions stayed higher than 1 billion.
