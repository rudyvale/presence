Solana Retreats Amid ETF Inflow Reversal

Presence Editorial · November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

On November 20, Solana declined by 6.1% to reach $68.68, effectively surrendering most of its recovery from a four-month low despite U.S. spot Solana funds recording inflows of nearly $12 million earlier in the week. During this downturn, the asset dipped as low as $67.80 on Binance, approaching its November low of $67.41.

Earlier data indicated that Bitwise contributed $10.8 million toward a total of $11.7 million flowing into U.S. spot Solana ETFs on November 16. Technical indicators showed Solana's daily RSI dropping to 23.52 as the price moved below the $72 level monitored by Trader Symba, while CoinGlass identified estimated liquidation levels clustered near $76–$77, situated just above the week's high.

Technical Indicators Signal Oversold Conditions

TradingView's Binance SOL/USDT chart recorded Solana at $68.68 at press time, marking a decline from a session high of $73.56 and placing the token roughly 36% below its October 21 close of $106.93. The asset lost $100 in value during late October before falling below $90 in early November, eventually touching $67.41 on November 15 to reach its lowest point in four months.

The broader market slide occurred alongside Bitcoin hitting $61,546 earlier on November 20 after dropping 10.8% over the preceding week. This cryptocurrency downturn followed U.S. technology stocks reversing early gains after Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, while U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded net withdrawals of $582.3 million in the previous session.

On November 16, Farside Investors logged $11.7 million in net inflows for American spot Solana exchange-traded funds, splitting the amount between Bitwise's BSOL at $10.8 million and VanEck's VSOL at $900,000. Subsequent trading days registered no net movement until November 19, when Grayscale's GSOL experienced a $1.1 million outflow.

This recent capital entry reversed a trend of six straight weeks of net withdrawals that extended through November 13, during which the funds shed approximately $197.4 million, with roughly $32 million leaving in the last week alone. While Farside's data indicated a cumulative net inflow of $10.6 million across the four sessions from November 16 to November 19, Solana's price recovery halted beneath the $76 mark before settling back into the upper $60s by Friday.

On X, Trader Symba identified a potential ascending triangle pattern on Solana's two-hour chart earlier on Nov. 20, noting a rising line connecting successive lows near $72 while rallies repeatedly stalled between $75 and $76. He suggested that a confirmed breakout above this resistance zone could initiate a stronger rally targeting the $78 to $80 range.

Following the subsequent Binance snapshot, SOL dropped beneath the established rising support zone, with its session low of $67.80 landing just 39 cents away from the Nov. 15 trough and aligning both lows within the $67–$68 range. To move higher, the asset needs to retake the $70 to $72 area before attempting another approach to the resistance level Symba had previously marked.

Technical indicators for Solana displayed pronounced weakness as the daily relative strength index declined to 23.52, falling below the conventional oversold threshold of 30. The daily MACD line registered a value of minus 7.67, which remained lower than its signal line at minus 7.06, while both lines stayed below zero and the histogram turned negative as prices returned near the previous weekend's low.

According to the Solana daily chart from TradingView dated November 21, the weekly timeframe recorded a decline of 5.57% with SOL trading at $68.72 while the candle remained open. Prior to this drop, the asset climbed to $75.78 earlier in the week, reaching a level previously marked as resistance by Symba on a two-hour analysis.

According to the Solana price weekly chart dated Nov. 21 from TradingView, the weekly Bollinger Bands positioned the 20-week moving average at $92.59 while placing the lower band at $58.95. Historical analysis of the daily chart indicates a previous swing low occurred in the low $60s during June, located between the November trough and that specific lower weekly band.

ETF Flows Shift from Inflows to Outflows

The Weekly ADX index registered a value of 23.93, staying slightly under the frequently monitored threshold of 25. Because this metric gauges trend strength whereas the price chart indicates direction, the weekly candle began at $72.77 and moved within an approximately $8 span by the snapshot moment.

CoinGlass data placed SOL at $68.63, a figure that matched observations from TradingView platforms. Liquidation zones above the current market price clustered between $69 and $72, with a denser grouping found near $76–$77, slightly exceeding the weekly peak. In contrast, the area below the present price featured sparser bands within the upper $60s, while higher levels on the chart revealed stronger signals around $91 and $100–$101. The most intense signal on the one-month view sat near $106, a position maintained since late October.
