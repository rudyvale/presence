A Curated Guide to Understanding and Building With NFTs

Sonal Chokshi · 12 min read

The NFT Canon is a useful reference for artists and creators, developers, corporations and institutions, communities and other organizations that want to understand or make more with non-fungible tokens.

It is a carefully selected set of readings and resources on everything NFTs, inspired by the a16z Crypto Canon, and it ranges from the big-picture view of what NFTs are and why they matter to how to mint, collect, and do more with them — including FAQs, ecosystem overviews, and applications like art, music, gaming, social tokens, creator DAOs, and others.

We’ll keep updating this as more people experiment, share their work, or publish resources for learning about NFTs. If you have suggestions for quality pieces to add, let us know @a16z.
