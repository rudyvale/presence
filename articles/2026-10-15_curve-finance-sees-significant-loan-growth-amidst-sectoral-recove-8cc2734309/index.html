Curve Finance Lending Surges as DeFi Sector Remains Stagnant

Presence Editorial · October 15, 2026 · 2 min read

Curve Finance reported a substantial increase of 68% in active loans to $70.9 million during Q3 2026, surpassing its previous year's closing balance. This growth contrasts with the overall DeFi lending sector, which has seen a decline from its 2025 levels.

The rise in Curve Finance’s active loans can be attributed to LlamaLend v2, which launched on Optimism and Ethereum mainnet. The team highlighted August as the period of strongest growth, driven by markets involving yield-bearing stablecoins.

Rapid Growth in Curve Finance Lending

Curve Finance noted that DeFi lending is recovering unevenly, with capital flowing towards assets that continue to generate yield while being used as collateral. This trend supports the team's strategy in expanding LlamaLend v2’s market offerings.

According to the source publication, the launch of LlamaLend v2 on Optimism included three distinct markets: ETH versus wstETH, wstETH versus USDC, and WBTC versus USDC. These new markets have since generated substantial activity, driving growth in Curve Finance’s lending operations.

Looking ahead into Q4, Curve Finance anticipates further market launches based on demand from asset issuers. A notable proposal involves a reUSD/sfrxUSD liquidity pool token market, allowing holders to borrow against their LP tokens without withdrawing assets.

This proposal is currently pending approval through a Curve DAO vote. The team believes this new market will extend borrowing access to holders of the specific reUSD/sfrxUSD pool position, enhancing liquidity and flexibility for users.

Proposed Market Expansion and Future Plans

Despite the sector-wide decline in active loans to $32.8 billion by September 2025, as reported, Curve Finance continues to see robust growth, driven by innovative lending solutions like LlamaLend v2 and strategic market expansions. The team remains optimistic about future developments.

Curve Finance’s focus on yield-bearing stablecoins and flexible collateral options underscores its commitment to supporting the evolving needs of DeFi users in a recovering market environment. The upcoming vote on the reUSD/sfrxUSD proposal will be crucial for future growth.
