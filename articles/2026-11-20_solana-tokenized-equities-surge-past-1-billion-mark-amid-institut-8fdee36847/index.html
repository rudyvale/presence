Solana Tokenized Equities Surge Past $1 Billion Mark Amid Institutional Expansion

Presence Editorial · November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

As additional financial institutions migrate stocks and exchange-traded funds to the blockchain, the total circulating value of Solana-based tokenized equities has climbed to $1.22 billion. This figure represents almost a two-fold increase compared to the amounts recorded by late October.

Cumulative trading volumes for these assets have now hit billions of dollars following moves by Ondo Finance, xStocks, and Backpack Securities to broaden their tokenized stock offerings on Solana. Earlier in the year, Securitize introduced tokenized U.S. equities to the network in October before growing its selection to 25 stocks by early November. In a future development scheduled for the second half of 2027, DTCC intends to link its tokenization service to Solana, which would enable eligible securities to be represented onchain.

Market Milestone: Value Doubles in Under Two Months

According to an announcement released by Solana on Nov. 19, citing data from Blockworks and Sam Schubert, the circulating value of tokenized equities on the network surpassed the $1 billion threshold for the first time. The current valuation stands at $1.22 billion, which represents nearly double the level recorded when October closed.

A visual representation demonstrates that circulating supply increased consistently across 2026, moving from under $200 million at the beginning of the period to roughly $600 million by September. The rate of expansion quickened in November, pushing the most recent figure to $1.22 billion following a stretch in October where values hovered close to the $600 million mark.

The circulating value of tokenized equities issued on Solana, which stands at $1.22 billion, is a specific measure that differs from the total trading volume these products have generated. This growth has largely centered on platforms including Ondo Finance, xStocks, and Backpack Securities, entities that have broadened the selection of traditional financial assets accessible via blockchain-based instruments.

According to an October ecosystem report released in November by the Solana Foundation, Ondo's tokenized assets on the network exceeded $500 million. Additionally, the report highlighted that decentralized exchange aggregator 1inch facilitated trading for over 450 Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs.

In January, Ondo launched more than 200 tokenized U.S. equities and exchange-traded funds on the Solana blockchain, with holdings connected to companies such as Nvidia, Apple, and Meta alongside prominent funds including SPY and QQQ. Subsequently, access was widened through wallet integrations after Exodus joined forces with Ondo in June, allowing eligible users to trade over 200 tokenized stocks, ETFs, and other real-world assets directly within its application.

Trading activity has increased alongside the growing product availability, with Solana's October report indicating cumulative xStocks trading volume reached $7.5 billion while Backpack Securities processed over $6 billion in tokenized asset trades. During the period from October through Sunrise, Backpack expanded its catalog by adding more than 20 tokenized stocks using its infrastructure partner.

In October, Securitize launched tokenized U.S. equities on the Solana network utilizing its registered broker-dealer framework. The initial release comprised 12 products associated with major firms such as Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Tesla, Amazon, and Strategy, permitting access for qualified investors across the United States, the European Union, and other authorized regions.

According to October reporting, Securitize confirmed that every token represents an underlying share incapable of being lent. Holders gain security entitlements with relevant economic advantages, such as dividends and voting privileges. On November 2, the firm broadened its selection by incorporating 13 additional U.S. stocks, raising the total catalog count to 25; new entries included Exxon Mobil, GameStop, and Uber.

Following the expansion of Solana's tokenized equities, Formation Securities announced in November its intention to launch a tokenized securities venue on the network in early 2027. This initiative aims to facilitate round-the-clock stock trading with initial offerings comprising shares of Exodus Movement, Forward Industries, and Galaxy Digital, executed via digital wallets and stablecoins. Concurrently, the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) plans to introduce tokenized securities to Solana in 2027 as part of its broader infrastructure development.

Platform Growth: New Issuers and Wallet Integrations Drive Adoption

On November 12, institutional interest in Solana's tokenization capabilities advanced when the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation revealed plans to connect its Tokenization Service directly to the network. Under this proposed integration, eligible securities currently held through the Depository Trust Company will be represented as blockchain-based tokens while maintaining their underlying ownership rights and investor protections.

On November 12, the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation revealed intentions to link its Tokenization Service with the Solana network as part of a broader multichain tokenization initiative expected to launch in the latter half of 2027. The organization reported that its subsidiaries handled securities transactions totaling $4.7 quadrillion in 2025, and its depository maintained holdings from over 150 countries worth $114 trillion combined.
