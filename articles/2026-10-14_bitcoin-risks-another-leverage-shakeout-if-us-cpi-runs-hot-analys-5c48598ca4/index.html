Analysts Flag Bitcoin Leverage Risk Ahead of U.S. CPI

Presence Editorial · October 14, 2026 · 2 min read

Analysts warned that a hotter U.S. CPI reading could trigger another Bitcoin selloff after a crypto liquidation wave totaling $811 million. Their assessment was made before the data release and described a conditional risk, rather than a confirmed market outcome.

The liquidation episode reduced market-wide open interest by only about 2%, leaving it at $140.9 billion. The analysts’ concern was that a large amount of exposure remained, so the selloff had not necessarily removed the conditions for another leverage-driven move.

Inflation data and market positioning

Bitget Research’s Ryan Lee pointed to the remaining leveraged exposure as a vulnerability around the release. A stronger inflation result or renewed pressure from Treasury yields could challenge market expectations; those conditions were presented as risks, rather than predictions of a completed decline.

The pre-release analysis put Bitcoin around $83,400–$83,500. It identified $81,000–$82,000 as an initial support area and the recent low near $80,400 as another downside reference. Those levels framed the discussion of potential price pressure.

Bitcoin ETF demand offered a separate signal. The assessment cited $175.7 million in inflows on October 13, following sizable outflows during the previous week. That rebound showed a return of inflows without resolving the separate question of leveraged exposure.

Flows and remaining leverage

ETF flows and open interest describe different parts of market positioning. A positive fund-flow figure does not establish that leverage has cleared, while a decline in open interest does not alone determine the direction of the next price move.

The warning remains tied to the inflation reading and the market’s response. The liquidation total, remaining open interest and renewed ETF inflows were elements of the pre-release assessment; none of them, on its own, confirmed that another selloff would occur.
