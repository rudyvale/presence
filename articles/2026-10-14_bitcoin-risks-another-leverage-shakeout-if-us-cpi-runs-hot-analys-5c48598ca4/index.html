Bitcoin Faces Inflation-Driven Risks as Open Interest Remains Elevated

Presence Editorial · October 14, 2026 · 3 min read

Bitcoin has narrowly avoided a significant selloff after an $811 million liquidation wave, leaving market-wide open interest at $140.9 billion despite a modest decline of about 2% in open interest levels post-selloff. This suggests substantial leveraged exposure remains.

Bitget Research’s Ryan Lee warns that an inflation surprise could trigger another round of liquidations as traders reassess Treasury yields and Federal Reserve policy. He cites elevated open interest as a key risk factor ahead of the U.S. Consumer Price Index release.

Post-Selloff Market Conditions

Lacie Zhang from Bitget Wallet highlighted $81,000–$82,000 support and a potential breakout above $84,500–$85,000 as critical levels to watch. She also noted persistent inflation could keep the Federal Reserve on a higher-for-longer path beyond current expectations.

With consumer demand showing resilience, retail sales excluding autos and gasoline rose by 4.05% year-on-year, according to Bitget Research’s analysis. New York Fed President John Williams indicated that further rate hikes may not be necessary in 2027.

The market remains cautious, with investors expecting one additional rate increase later this year but wary of persistent inflation pushing the Federal Reserve to maintain a higher interest-rate environment. Bulls would regain clearer control through sustained price moves above $84,500–$85,000.

Open interest levels suggest that substantial leveraged exposure remains in the market, which could be vulnerable to further adjustments if inflation surprises traders. The upcoming U.S. Consumer Price Index release is a key risk point for Bitcoin's price action.

Traders will closely monitor consumer demand indicators and Fed policy signals in the coming weeks to gauge the direction of market sentiment and potential forced position closures. The current environment remains highly sensitive to inflation data.

While a softer-than-expected inflation report could lead to a sharp rebound in Bitcoin prices, any hotter-than-expected readings could trigger another round of liquidations. The market's resilience will be tested as it awaits further economic data and policy announcements.

The ongoing leverage exposure and the potential for inflation-driven adjustments highlight the volatility risks facing Bitcoin in the near term. Analysts advise traders to remain vigilant and prepared for market shifts based on upcoming economic data releases.

Despite recent selloffs, Bitcoin's resilience is evident from its ability to maintain significant open interest levels. However, the market remains susceptible to further adjustments if inflation pressures continue to build or if the Federal Reserve signals a more aggressive stance.

Expert Analysis on Inflation Risks

The current market environment underscores the importance of careful risk management and strategic positioning for both retail and institutional investors. The upcoming U.S. Consumer Price Index release will be a critical factor in shaping short-term price movements.

As the market awaits further economic data and policy announcements, analysts recommend maintaining a cautious approach to trading strategies. The interplay between inflation expectations and Federal Reserve actions will continue to influence Bitcoin's performance in the coming weeks.
