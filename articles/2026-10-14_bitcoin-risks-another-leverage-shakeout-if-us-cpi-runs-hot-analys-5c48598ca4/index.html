Bitcoin Faces Inflation-Driven Selloff Risk

Presence Editorial · October 14, 2026 · 2 min read

Bitcoin has faced significant selling pressure, with an $811 million liquidation wave leaving market-wide open interest down only about 2% at $140.9 billion before the U.S. Consumer Price Index (CPI) release. Analysts warn of potential further declines if inflation data surprises markets.

Bitget Research’s Ryan Lee highlighted that elevated open interest suggests substantial leveraged exposure remains, potentially fueling another decline should the CPI report an unexpected rise in inflation or Treasury yields. He advises traders to reassess their positions ahead of the release.

Market Concerns Over Inflation Data

Zhang from Bitget Research assessed a 77% probability of a Fed hold in October but warned that higher-than-expected core CPI readings or renewed Treasury yield pressure could lead to sharper market adjustments. She expects one more rate increase this year.

Sondergaard noted Bitcoin ETFs attracted $175.7 million on Oct. 13 after significant outflows during the previous week, indicating some selective positioning rather than broad market confidence. He forecasts headline inflation at 3.6% and core CPI at 2.5%.

Ahead of the CPI release, Bitcoin traded around $83,400–$83,500 with initial support at $81,000–$82,000 and a downside reference near the recent low of $80,400. Zhang identified core inflation below 0.2% month-on-month as key for a bullish scenario.

The main factors to monitor include December Fed expectations, ETF flows, and continued institutional accumulation. Analysts advise traders to prepare for potential market adjustments based on the upcoming CPI data release and other economic indicators.

ETF Flows and Institutional Interest

While Bitcoin has shown resilience in recent weeks, analysts caution that leverage remains a significant risk factor. The market's response to inflation data could lead to further volatility as traders reassess their positions and expectations.

Despite some positive ETF inflows, the overall sentiment in the market is cautious ahead of the CPI release. Traders are advised to stay informed on key economic indicators and adjust their strategies accordingly to mitigate potential risks.
