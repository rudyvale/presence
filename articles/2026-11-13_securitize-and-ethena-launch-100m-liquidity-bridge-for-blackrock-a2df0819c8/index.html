Securitize and Ethena Launch $100M Liquidity Bridge for BlackRock's BUIDL

Presence Editorial · November 13, 2026 · 4 min read

Securitize introduced a new liquidity arrangement offering up to $100 million in capacity for those holding BlackRock's BUIDL fund, enabling exchanges to utilize supported stablecoins even when traditional markets are closed with assistance from Ethena. The starting pool includes $50 million split evenly between USDC and BUIDL, while an additional $50 million could be unlocked within an hour depending on capacity and terms.

Approved participants may conduct exchanges in either direction, even on weekends when standard markets are shut. On Nov. 12, Securitize revealed the initiative, outlining a platform for authorized users to transfer between BlackRock's USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund and compatible stablecoins such as USDC. Ethena independently verified its backing of this facility, which is managed by Securitize.

Facility Launch and Initial Capital Deployment

This new launch provides fund token holders with an additional mechanism to convert their assets into stablecoins, bypassing the need to wait for banking and settlement systems to resume operations. The same infrastructure also enables investors to transfer supported stablecoins back into BUIDL.

Securitize's published breakdown indicates that at the time of launch, the facility contained $25 million in USDC and $25 million in BUIDL. As this initial liquidity is deployed, an additional $50 million can be made available within roughly an hour, contingent upon the facility's specific terms, capacity limits, and asset availability. The cited total capacity of $100 million encompasses both the initial holdings and the additional liquidity that can be accessed. This figure does not signify that $100 million in stablecoins is sitting idle and ready for immediate withdrawal by holders, nor does it represent an amount that remains continuously funded.

According to the related disclosure from Securitize, usage of this facility is confined to qualified BUIDL owners and hinges on specific conditions, defined timeframes for transactions, and necessary operational standards. Claims regarding continuous operation should not be interpreted as a promise that all requests will be fulfilled instantly or that the entire resource pool remains perpetually accessible.

A review of the RWA.xyz interface showed BUIDL's aggregate holdings near $1.66 billion at the time of this assessment. Relative to that total, the facility's peak potential represents about 6 percent of the fund, whereas the starting USDC stockpile accounts for roughly 1.5 percent. These ratios are calculated based on the fluctuating value displayed by the platform.

Securitize stated in its announcement thread that tokenized assets are capable of moving across blockchain networks at any time, contrasting this with traditional markets where liquidity is constrained by banking hours and settlement infrastructure. The facility is specifically designed to provide eligible investors with access over weekends. Guy Young, founder of Ethena Labs, commented on the arrangement in a statement shared by Securitize, noting that digital assets operate around the clock and that institutional liquidity needs must keep pace with this reality.

Guy Young, founder of Ethena Labs, noted in a statement shared by Securitize that digital assets function continuously while institutional liquidity must adapt to this rhythm. He emphasized that the new setup bridges the divide between standard banking hours and blockchain operations, thereby increasing the utility of tokenized holdings for institutional players. The investor criteria for BUIDL are distinct from the onchain transfer capabilities; RWA.xyz designates the fund for U.S. qualified purchasers. The onboarding process involves identity verification, execution of subscription agreements, and transfer agent approval prior to whitelisting an investor.

Securitize noted that the existing collaboration between the companies, which operates through USDtb—a digital dollar product primarily backed by BUIDL—serves as the foundation for this latest launch. As previously reported in June 2025, round-the-clock USDtb swaps enabled qualified users onboarded via Securitize to exchange between BUIDL and USDtb using a liquidity fund smart contract, a capability introduced on June 18.

By March 2025, Ethena had already become a substantial BUIDL investor after a $200 million allocation helped push the fund's assets above $1 billion. BUIDL provides investors with exposure to cash, U.S. Treasuries, and repurchase agreements via tokenized fund shares.

Operational Scope and Market Timing Benefits

Prior to the recent facility launch, other liquidity mechanisms for BlackRock's BUIDL were already operational. In February, Uniswap Labs and Securitize established trading via UniswapX, utilizing a request-for-quote system that sourced prices from approved market participants including Flowdesk, Tokka Labs, and Wintermute, with all trades settling atomically onchain.

BlackRock is examining new ways to apply tokenized funds as collateral inside traditional financial systems. During an October discussion, Dan Maley, who leads cash management for BlackRock in the Asia Pacific region, outlined methods to move fund holdings without converting them into cash first, highlighting the difficulties of bringing digital-asset structures into conventional finance. This institutional setup traces back to November 2025, when Securitize revealed that qualified Binance traders were permitted to use BUIDL as collateral outside of an exchange.
