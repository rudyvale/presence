Ethereum Surges as NFT Market Hits $33M Weekly High

Presence Editorial · November 22, 2026 · 5 min read

NFT sales volume increased by 3.88% to reach $33 million over the seven-day period ending Nov. 21, with Ethereum dominating network activity while a Bitcoin collectible achieved the week's peak price. Ethereum accounted for $12.91 million in total sales, whereas Polygon, ranking second, experienced an 8.01% decline in its trading volume. Within specific collections, two Alchemix V3 Transmuter transactions comprised nearly 65% of that group's weekly market activity.

Data from CryptoSlam indicates that buyer addresses grew by 60.89% to total 109,319, while seller addresses rose by 68.95% to reach 85,937. Conversely, the number of transactions decreased by 3.63% to 578,892, resulting in an average recorded sale price of approximately $57, which represents an increase from roughly $53 in the prior comparison period. The highest individual asset value recorded was Bitcoin's $X@AI at $381,783, followed by a Mutant Ape priced at $342,251.

Market Leaders: Ethereum Dominates Volume Amidst Rising Addresses

Data from CryptoSlam recorded a 2.56% decline in NFT sales during the November 14 snapshot, a period when both buyer and seller addresses decreased by more than 70% on the platform's rolling comparison. A dashboard released on November 21 indicated that counts for both buyer and seller addresses increased even as transaction volumes continued to fall; these figures represent unique addresses rather than individual people, meaning one person can trade through multiple wallets.

Sales of NFTs on Ethereum rose by 33.50% to reach $12.91 million, representing approximately 39% of the total market volume. CryptoSlam tracked 13,108 buyer addresses on the Ethereum network, a figure more than double that of the comparison period. Ethereum had also ranked as the top blockchain in the October 31 rankings during a week when marketwide sales dropped 35.37% to $42.13 million.

Ethereum dominated the weekly NFT market with sales totaling $12.91 million, a 33.50% increase from the prior period, while activity declined on Polygon and Bitcoin according to CryptoSlam data. Polygon secured second place with $7.46 million in genuine sales and 33,053 distinct buyer addresses, reflecting a 62.73% rise; however, the platform also recorded $17.55 million in trades flagged as wash volume, bringing its combined total to $25.01 million. In contrast, Ethereum's dashboard identified only $51,379 in wash volume for the week.

Bitcoin ranked third with $3.87 million in sales despite a 20.72% decrease, even as buyer addresses surged 87.16% to reach 6,489. Immutable trailed at $2.30 million following a 16.67% drop, while Base surpassed BNB Chain in dollar volume with sales climbing 31.12% to $1.93 million. BNB Chain experienced a decline of 20.67% to $1.90 million, and Solana placed seventh with $1.15 million in sales and an increase of 2.91%, recording 23,293 buyer addresses.

Courtyard led the weekly collection rankings on Polygon with $6.35 million in sales, representing approximately 85% of the network's reported volume despite a 7.71% decline from the prior period. The collection processed 125,328 transactions averaging roughly $51 each, drawing 19,099 buyer addresses which decreased by 3.35%, while seller addresses increased by 12.63% to reach a total of 16,841.

Bored Ape Yacht Club secured second place with $1.85 million in sales following a 5.20% rise, though its transaction count climbed 18.05% to 157 as buyer addresses fell 18.09% to 77. Guild of Guardians Heroes finished third with $1.22 million after an 18.71% drop, generating revenue from 914 transactions while maintaining sales above the $1 million threshold.

CryptoPunks generated $1,040,000 in value through 19 trades, reflecting a 24.04% increase in dollar terms compared to the prior period. Alchemix V3 Transmuter recorded sales of $836,442 from eight transactions, representing an increase of more than sixteenfold in total value. Mutant Ape Yacht Club concluded the top tier with $834,964 across 303 transactions, marking a rise of 113.53% in sales volume.

Two Bitcoin-based collections completed the top eight rankings, with $FTSS achieving $766,799 and $ATMC reaching $681,169. The $FTSS collection saw its sales climb by 135.46%, while $ATMC experienced a 27.58% increase despite an overall decline in Bitcoin NFT volume for the week. These two BRC-20 collections processed 871 and 706 transactions respectively, contributing to their respective valuation growth.

According to data from CryptoSlam, a $X@AI BRC-20 entry topped the individual rankings by trading for 5.9916 BTC, equivalent to $381,782.80. Mutant Ape Yacht Club #1134 secured second place with a sale of 189.65 ETH valued at $342,250.84, representing approximately 41% of the collection's weekly dollar volume.

A Bitcoin $X@AI BRC-20 collectible topped the week's individual NFT sales at $381,783 | Source — CryptoSlam Alchemix V3 Transmuter #285 and #290 occupied the next two positions in the rankings, selling for $275,566.28 and $266,169.13 in wrapped ether respectively, combining for a total of $541,735.41. Additionally, Alchemix V3 Transmuter #260 from the same collection led the Nov. 7 rankings at a price just over $1.04 million.

Collection Rankings: Courtyard Tops Polygon While Bored Ape Yacht Club Clim

Outside the top eight rankings, CryptoSlam's network identified Momo #51 as the highest-priced sale on Polygon for $100,000. Okay Bear #2226 led the Solana highlights with a transaction of $19,102, while an Erling Haaland collectible topped Panini's list at $10,000.

Async Art #199 sold for approximately $210,870 in USDC to secure fifth place, followed by VeeFriends #9573 which fetched $201,750 paid in 111.11 ETH. Forest Road Facility #15 changed hands for $173,045 in USDC, and Skulls of Luci #24 completed the top eight with a $160,206 sale settled in the same stablecoin.
