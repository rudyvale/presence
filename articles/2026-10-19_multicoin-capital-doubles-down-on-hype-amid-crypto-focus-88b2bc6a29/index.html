Multicoin Capital Doubles Down on HYPE Amid Crypto Focus

Presence Editorial · October 19, 2026 · 4 min read

Hyperliquid’s HYPE has emerged as the largest holding in Multicoin Capital's hedge fund, though the exact size of this position remains undisclosed. The firm has maintained its commitment to crypto assets rather than pivoting towards standalone artificial intelligence investments.

In June, Multicoin had already highlighted HYPE among its significant liquid-fund investments. More recent September wallet reports have noted transfers linked to Multicoin moving to Coinbase Prime, although these do not confirm any completed sales.

Multicoin's Emphasis on Crypto Over AI

According to Bloomberg, HYPE is now the largest holding in Multicoin Capital's portfolio. Spencer Applebaum, who serves as a general partner and co-head of venture investments at Multicoin, confirmed this without providing specifics on the token’s size within the fund. This comes after Paradigm decided to broaden its investment scope to include artificial intelligence and robotics while maintaining focus on cryptocurrencies.

Applebaum explained in an interview with Bloomberg that Multicoin acquired its stake in HYPE through public market transactions and typically maintains such positions for several years. He emphasized the firm’s expertise in digital assets and indicated that Multicoin will continue to invest in this area, while expressing skepticism towards efforts to link blockchain technology directly with artificial intelligence.

Multicoin Capital disclosed its significant investment in HYPE through a June 25 post, revealing an initial large position taken early in 2026 and subsequent accumulation. The firm highlighted Hyperliquid as a high-speed trading platform for blockchain and decentralized exchange purposes, with plans to expand into other financial markets beyond cryptocurrency derivatives.

The source publication reported that Multicoin’s valuation model from June projected a $319 price target for HYPE by 2028 under its base case scenario. The firm's investment rationale was based on Hyperliquid’s performance in 2025, which included $873 million in revenue and $2.9 trillion in trading volume. Additionally, the platform saw user growth from around 301,000 to 923,000 users, with open interest reaching $6 billion during that period.

In June, Multicoin Capital highlighted HYPE as a significant position in its liquid fund portfolio, though the exact weight was not disclosed. The firm's research report at the time projected an annual earnings figure of approximately $8 billion by 2028 for Hyperliquid, applying a valuation multiple of 20 times to arrive at a $160 billion enterprise value.

The same June report outlined both a bearish scenario with a price target of $109 and a bullish case setting the price at $689. These projections were contingent on varying assumptions regarding derivatives market growth and decentralized exchanges' market share, representing investment scenarios rather than updated forecasts or realized returns as of October. Additionally, Multicoin identified several risks associated with HYPE, including decentralization challenges, regulatory uncertainties, competitive pressures, interoperability issues between HyperCore and HyperEVM, potential token dilution, and the risk of bad debt due to illiquid markets and liquidation gaps.

Multicoin Capital's investment thesis for HYPE includes a focus on HIP-3 markets such as commodities and equity indexes, alongside opportunities in prediction markets, options, and portfolio margining. The firm acknowledges potential regulatory and technical risks associated with these areas. On September 24, Lookonchain attributed a deposit of 130,331 HYPE tokens, valued at $12.15 million, to Multicoin Capital on Coinbase.

According to analytics firm Lookonchain, since July 28, Multicoin has transferred 4.23 million HYPE tokens worth approximately $285 million into Coinbase Prime for institutional custody and execution services. This transfer does not confirm whether Multicoin sold the tokens afterward or maintained them as part of its portfolio. The available data does not provide a complete balance sheet or reconcile purchases, withdrawals, and remaining holdings, so it is unclear if Multicoin has continuously expanded its position.

Institutional interest in Hyperliquid's HYPE token was evident when Nasdaq-listed treasury company Hyperliquid Strategies purchased 494,200 HYPE tokens worth $45.8 million over a span of 16 hours on September 25, according to Lookonchain’s analysis. This purchase followed an earlier accumulation of 5.51 million HYPE tokens valued at approximately $476 million over the previous month.

As of June 30, Multicoin Capital reported holding around 29.3 million HYPE tokens worth $1.9 billion after raising $647 million in equity capital. In comparison, Paradigm announced on July 8 that it had raised a fourth fund with commitments totaling $1.2 billion.

Multicoin Capital recently outlined its investment strategy, which includes backing companies developing markets and financial systems across various sectors such as crypto, AI, robotics, and other technology fields. The firm highlighted Hyperliquid, Kalshi, and Tempo as examples of this approach.

HYPE's Growth and Investment Risks

In the same period, Framework Ventures announced a $400 million fourth fund in June to support startups in crypto, AI, robotics, and energy. Additionally, Haun Ventures secured $1 billion in May for investments in crypto infrastructure, tokenization, and AI agents.

Multicoin Capital has outlined its investment strategy, which includes backing companies that are developing markets and financial systems across various technology fields such as crypto, AI, robotics, and others. Notably, the firm highlighted Hyperliquid, Kalshi, and Tempo as examples of this approach. Meanwhile, other firms like Framework Ventures and Haun Ventures have also secured significant funding for similar ventures, with Framework raising $400 million in June to support startups in areas including crypto, AI, robotics, and energy, while Haun Ventures raised $1 billion in May specifically for investments in crypto infrastructure, tokenization, and AI agents.
