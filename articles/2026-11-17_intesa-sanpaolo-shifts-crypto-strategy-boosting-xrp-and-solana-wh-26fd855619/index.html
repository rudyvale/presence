Intesa Sanpaolo Shifts Crypto Strategy: Boosting XRP and Solana While Trimming Bitcoin

Presence Editorial · November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Italy's largest banking group, Intesa Sanpaolo, increased its disclosed crypto ETF holdings to $108.56 million during the third quarter of 2026, reflecting a net rise of $17.71 million from the prior period.

The Italian bank expanded its specific asset allocations by building a Solana position worth $14.14 million and raising its XRP exposure to $27.21 million by September, while simultaneously reducing its ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF holdings by nearly 43% and selling 198,010 shares of its Ethereum ETF; additionally, the institution established a new Hyperliquid ETF position valued at approximately $599,000.

Altcoin Gains Drive Portfolio Expansion

Data from the bank's most recent Form 13F submission to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission indicates that its crypto-linked securities totaled $90.84 million as of June's close, followed by a third-quarter increase of $17.71 million. Signed on November 16, this disclosure details holdings as of September 30 and reveals expanded stakes in funds connected to XRP and Solana, alongside a reduced allocation to a Hyperliquid product.

Bitcoin continues to represent the bank's primary crypto exposure at $61.4 million, despite a decline from $69.32 million in the prior quarter, while Ethereum holdings decreased from $7.1 million to $5.21 million. A significant shift involved the bank's stake in the Bitwise Solana Staking ETF, which expanded from seven shares at June's end to 875,407 shares by September.

According to the disclosed ETF portfolio from Intesa Sanpaolo, Solana holdings reached a valuation of $14.14 million, positioning the asset as the third-largest cryptocurrency within the bank's holdings. This standing differs from an earlier period in March when the institution possessed 2,817 shares of that specific product before scaling back its stake to nearly zero by the second quarter.

Regarding XRP exposure, Intesa maintained its existing holding of 712,319 shares in the Grayscale XRP Trust ETF while simultaneously establishing a new position comprising 402,750 shares in the Bitwise XRP ETF. The combined value of these investments reached $27.21 million at the end of September, representing an increase from $14.42 million recorded three months earlier.

Intesa Sanpaolo increased its exposure to XRP by adding a new holding valued at approximately $6.7 million through the Bitwise XRP ETF, while the remainder of the portfolio growth stemmed from a higher reported valuation of its pre-existing Grayscale position. The bank has consistently utilized exchange-traded investment products to secure access to cryptocurrencies, building upon a substantial XRP investment via Grayscale that was disclosed earlier in the year.

Conversely, the institution significantly reduced its largest position by trimming holdings in the ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF from 3,473,584 shares recorded in June down to 1,984,024 shares as of September. This adjustment represented a decrease of 1,489,560 shares, or roughly 42.9%, leaving a remaining position worth $54.94 million compared to the $67.63 million value held in the previous quarter.

Intesa Sanpaolo raised its holdings in XRP and Solana ETFs while simultaneously adjusting positions in other digital asset funds, with its stake in BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust moving in the opposite direction by climbing from 40,723 shares to 126,448 shares. Ethereum holdings were reduced through the iShares Staked Ethereum Trust ETF, where the bank lowered its position from 349,600 shares to 151,590 shares, cutting 198,010 shares during the quarter. Another new investment appeared in the Bitwise Hyperliquid ETF, where Intesa reported 12,400 shares valued at $599,037.

The regulatory filing details the bank's U.S.-listed securities, including cryptocurrency ETFs, within a broader portfolio of 243 positions totaling roughly $2.98 billion. In addition to digital asset funds, Intesa Sanpaolo continues to hold stakes in public firms involved in trading, custody, stablecoins, and tokenization services. At the close of September, Robinhood represented its most significant crypto-linked equity stake, worth about $3.9 million.

Bitcoin and Ethereum Positions Shrink Sharply

Intesa Sanpaolo disclosed stakes in Hyperliquid Strategies, which manages HYPE treasury assets, alongside Avalanche Treasury Corp. Additional investments comprised Coinbase valued at $652,000, Figure at $611,000, Securitize near $480,000, and Eightco at $468,000. Circle trailed these with a $1.88 million position, whereas BitGo, the digital asset custodian, held $1.48 million.

Peers within Italy's banking sector are also assessing digital asset prospects. During September, UniCredit started investigating infrastructure for crypto custody and brokerage, potentially covering tokenized investments and stablecoins. The lender had earlier launched a structured product for professional clients tied to BlackRock's Bitcoin ETF and released a minibond on the public blockchain.
