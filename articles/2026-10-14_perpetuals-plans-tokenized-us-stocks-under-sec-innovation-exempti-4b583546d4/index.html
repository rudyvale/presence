Perpetuals Aims to Offer Tokenized U.S. Stocks via SEC Innovation Exemption

Presence Editorial · October 14, 2026 · 2 min read

Perpetuals.com has announced plans to tokenize U.S. stocks under the SEC’s five-year Innovation Exemption, subjecting its launch to a 30-day notice period and adherence to specific conditions set by the framework. The company aims to provide blockchain-based access to U.S. equities while preserving dividend and voting rights.

The announcement follows Perpetuals.com Ltd’s Oct. 14 disclosure on Nasdaq under ticker PDC, which outlined the conditions for its tokenized securities offering. The company highlighted potential factors affecting the launch, including regulatory developments and market conditions.

SEC's Temporary Exemption for Tokenized Securities

Perpetuals has outlined plans for traders to gain access to U.S. equities through blockchain-based tokens, featuring round-the-clock trading, near-instant settlement, and fractional ownership. The company assured that users would maintain full dividend and voting rights, meeting SEC requirements for equivalent conventional shares.

For qualifying products, the Securities and Exchange Commission mandates that venues must ensure token holders have equivalent rights to traditional stock owners, with trading paused when the underlying stock is halted on its primary exchange. Perpetuals highlights its extensive experience in tokenization.

The company’s expansion into U.S. markets underscores its commitment to leveraging blockchain technology for financial innovation. Perpetuals has developed pre-IPO contracts linked to notable companies such as Coinbase, Airbnb, and Robinhood since 2021, according to the release.

The launch of tokenized U.S. equities is subject to a 30-day notice period after the publication of Perpetuals’ venue notice, with compliance and market conditions as key factors influencing its success. The company remains optimistic about user adoption and regulatory developments.

Tokenization Features and Regulatory Compliance

Perpetuals.com’s expansion into U.S. markets is part of a broader strategy to leverage blockchain technology for financial innovation, aligning with the SEC’s temporary exemption framework. The company aims to provide traders with enhanced access and flexibility in trading U.S. equities.

While Perpetuals’ plans are subject to regulatory approval and market conditions, the company remains confident in its ability to deliver on its vision of tokenized U.S. stocks through blockchain technology. The launch is expected to provide traders with round-the-clock trading, near-instant settlement, and fractional ownership.
